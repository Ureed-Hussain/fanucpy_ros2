# Code of conduct

This project is intended to help students learn and collaborate safely. Be
respectful, constructive, and patient. Discuss technical decisions with
evidence, welcome questions from less experienced contributors, and avoid
harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or disclosure of private lab
information.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer whenever possible.
